How it teaches

It asks before it answers. The tutor starts from what you think is happening.

Hints come in stages. A nudge first, then help with the approach, then a worked example from a different problem that you still have to adapt.

Nothing rushes you. If you stop making progress, the tutor offers a smaller step or explains the idea another way.

Eighteen ways to make a lesson easier to use.

Dyslexia font, contrast, text size, line spacing, reading mask, read aloud, focus mode, calm mode, reduced motion, voice input, and more. They open from any lesson and stay on between sessions.

Some people need these. Other people just find that a long session gets easier.
