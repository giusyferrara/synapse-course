Privacy Policy

What personal data Synapse collects, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have.

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data Synapse collects, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have. Synapse is a learning platform operated by GyEffe Technologies Ltd (company number SC899700), a private limited company registered in Scotland, registered office 5 South Charlotte Street, Edinburgh, EH2 4AN. We are the controller of the data below. For any privacy question, contact hello@gyeffe.co.uk.

1What Synapse is

Synapse is a web platform for learning to code and program — interactive lessons and exercises, a sandboxed code runner, flashcards and quizzes, and an AI tutor. It is open to everyone; it contains no mature or adult content.

2The data we collect

  • Account data — your email address and display name, used to create and secure your account.
  • Learning data — your progress, lessons, exercises, quiz and flashcard activity.
  • Code & tutor content — the code you write and run in the sandbox, the messages you send to the AI tutor, and the AI's responses, stored so the platform can give continuity and a personalised experience.
  • Research data — voluntary only — if, and only if, you choose to opt in to the research programme, we collect the additional study data described at opt-in. Participation is entirely optional and separate from using Synapse (see section 5).
  • Technical data — basic device and diagnostic information needed to run the platform and keep it secure.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without appropriate consent, and we apply high-privacy defaults.

3How we use your data

To provide and operate the service: to run the AI tutor, execute your code safely, save your progress, keep the platform secure, and — only with your opt-in — for research. We do not sell your personal data.

4AI processing and third-party providers

To generate tutoring, the content you send — including your questions and the code in your editor — is transmitted to third-party AI providers, which may include Anthropic, OpenAI and Google. They process the content to return a result under their own terms and privacy policies. The tutor is artificial intelligence, not a real person.

5Research (voluntary)

Synapse has a research programme, but taking part is voluntary and opt-in: you can use every feature without joining, and you can withdraw at any time. Aggregated or anonymised research outputs — that can no longer identify you — may be published or archived (for example under a Zenodo DOI). To join, leave, or ask about the research, email synapse_4ai@outlook.com.

6Sharing

We share data only with the providers needed to run the service — hosting and the AI providers named above. We may also disclose data where required by law or to protect users.

7Legal bases (UK/EU GDPR)

Where the UK GDPR and, where applicable, the EU GDPR apply, we process data on the basis of: performing our contract with you (providing the platform); your consent (for example, sending content to AI providers, and joining research); and our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and working. You can withdraw consent at any time.

8Payments

Synapse is currently free to use. We do not take payments, and we do not receive or store your card details. If we introduce paid plans, we will update this policy — naming the payment processor and describing the limited data it handles — before any charge is made.

9Retention

We keep your data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time (see section 10); residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for up to 30 days, and we keep only what the law requires.

10Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can access, correct, delete, or port your data, and object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any right, email hello@gyeffe.co.uk. In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO); in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

11Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit and a sandboxed environment for running your code. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

12Changes & contact

We may update this policy; when we make material changes we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Questions: hello@gyeffe.co.uk.
