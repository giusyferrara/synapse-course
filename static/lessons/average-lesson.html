The question

Beating your own average

You keep a note of how many pages you read each day. On how many days did you read more than your average up to that point — counting only the days before, not the day itself?

The first day never counts: there's nothing behind it to beat.

pages = [12, 20, 16, 30, 18]
answer = 2

The rules. Between 0 and 1000 days. Each day is a whole number of pages, at least one. No days at all means the answer is 0. And more than means strictly more — matching your average is not beating it.
